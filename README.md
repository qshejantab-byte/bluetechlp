# BlueTech Vision

I want you to build a completely new, premium, world-class landing page for BLUE TECH.

IMPORTANT:

This is a NEW PROJECT.

Do not assume you know anything about previous projects.

Do not reuse the structure, copy, components, branding, or content from another website.

However, use the following existing BlueTech website as your source of truth for understanding the company:

EXISTING WEBSITE:

https://blue-tech.onrender.com/

Your first responsibility is to study the existing website carefully and understand:

- BlueTech's actual business

- Its actual products

- Product categories

- Brands

- Services

- Existing company information

- Contact information

- Address

- Phone numbers

- Email addresses

- Existing visual identity

- Logo

- Brand colors

- Existing terminology

DO NOT invent products, brands, statistics, addresses, phone numbers, certifications, partnerships, or company claims that are not supported by the existing BlueTech website or information explicitly provided later.

If something is unclear, preserve it rather than inventing it.

==================================================

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

==================================================

Create a high-end technology company landing page for BlueTech.

BlueTech is a RESELLER of technology products.

BlueTech is NOT a distributor.

Do not describe BlueTech as:

- an exclusive distributor

- an authorized distributor

- a manufacturer

- a sole distributor

unless the existing website explicitly confirms such a claim.

The website should position BlueTech as a trusted technology reseller and solutions provider.

The most important product category should be:

INTERACTIVE SMART DISPLAYS / INTERACTIVE SCREENS.

However, BlueTech sells other technology products as well.

Therefore, this website must NOT feel like a single-product landing page.

It should feel like:

BLUE TECH

A modern technology company with interactive displays as one of its flagship product categories.

The interactive screen should receive the strongest visual and marketing emphasis.

Other products should support the broader BlueTech technology ecosystem.

==================================================

CORE BRAND POSITIONING

==================================================

The visitor should understand the company within a few seconds:

BLUE TECH

Technology that helps people work, learn, communicate and operate better.

Interactive smart displays should be presented as the flagship product category.

The overall brand should communicate:

- Modern technology

- Reliability

- Professionalism

- Innovation

- Practical solutions

- Quality products

- Trust

- Local availability

- Customer support

Avoid making BlueTech look like a small electronics shop.

Avoid making it look like a generic IT company.

Avoid making it look like a cheap online store.

The goal is to position BlueTech as a serious, modern technology solutions and product company.

16 — MOBILE EXPERIENCE

==================================================

Do not treat mobile as a smaller desktop.

Design the mobile experience intentionally.

375px

390px

414px

430px

The mobile experience must feel premium.

Requirements:

- zero horizontal scrolling

- no clipped content

- no overlapping elements

- no oversized typography

- no tiny buttons

- no awkward image cropping

- no excessive empty space

- no broken animations

- no desktop layout squeezed into mobile

HERO MOBILE:

The product must remain visually dominant.

Headline should remain readable.

CTA buttons should be thumb-friendly.

Prefer:

[Explore Interactive Displays]

[Get a Quote]

MOBILE NAV:

Use a polished hamburger menu.

The official BlueTech logo must remain visible.

MOBILE PRODUCT SHOWCASE:

MOBILE FEATURE SECTIONS:

MOBILE CTA:

23 — FINAL DESIGN STANDARD

==================================================

Before considering the project complete, perform a full design audit.

Ask:

Does this look like a premium technology company?

Does the interactive display immediately stand out?

Does the visitor understand what BlueTech sells?

Does the website still communicate that BlueTech sells other technology products?

Does BlueTech look like a reseller rather than a distributor?

Does the page feel trustworthy?

Does it look professionally designed rather than AI-generated?

Does mobile feel as intentionally designed as desktop?

Are all contact details accurate?

Are all products and claims based on actual BlueTech information?

Is the website fast?

Are the CTAs clear?

Is the page visually memorable?

If any answer is NO, refine the design before finishing.
